Privacy policy

Dummy, a personal assistant. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Who uses it

Dummy is used only by its owner, Prince Panwar, with his own Google account. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Dummy can read and organise his Gmail (gmail.modify) and read his Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It uses the Gmail permission only to mark messages as read, archive newsletters and add labels. It never sends or deletes email, and it cannot change calendar events.

How the data is used

  • To notify the owner of new email (sender, subject and a short preview) and upcoming meetings, by Telegram message or phone call to the owner.
  • To read or summarise a specific email when the owner asks.
  • To keep the owner's inbox tidy: marking old or bulk mail as read, archiving newsletters and labelling important mail.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the owner's private server. Only message IDs and reminder status are kept, so the same email or meeting is not reported twice. Email and calendar data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the owner. Access tokens are stored on that server with owner-only file permissions.

Dummy's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
